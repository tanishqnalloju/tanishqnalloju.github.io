Typography in the Age of AI

When models generate the words, the craft of setting type matters more — not less.

AI can draft a paragraph in seconds. It cannot decide whether that paragraph should whisper or shout on the page.

Hierarchy is still human work

Type scale, weight, and measure are product decisions. They tell the reader what to trust first.

Sample type hierarchy on a cream canvas

Practical rules that still hold

  • Prefer one display face and one text face for a portfolio or product surface
  • Keep line length around 60–75 characters for body copy
  • Use space before you use a second accent color

Where models help

Drafts, alt text, and first-pass documentation benefit from automation. Final rhythm — the pause between heading and body — is still a designer’s ear.

Typography is how we respect the reader’s time when the words are cheap.
